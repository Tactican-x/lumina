# ✨ Lumina

### Build better habits. Achieve meaningful goals. Take control of your finances.

Lumina is an all-in-one personal growth and money management application designed to help users organise their daily lives, develop better habits, accomplish personal goals, and manage their finances.

By combining productivity tools with personal finance tracking, Lumina provides a unified space for building consistency, monitoring progress, and making more intentional decisions.

---

## 🌱 Personal Growth & Productivity

Lumina provides tools to help users build routines, stay organised, and make consistent progress towards their goals.

### 1. Habit Tracking
- Create and manage personal habits.
- Track daily habit completion.
- Monitor consistency and progress.
- Build sustainable routines.
- Review personal habit activity.

### 2. Goal Management
- Set personal goals.
- Organise goals and track their progress.
- Monitor milestones and achievements.
- Review progress towards personal objectives.

### 3. Task Management
- Create and organise tasks.
- Manage daily responsibilities.
- Track task completion.
- Stay focused on important activities.

### 4. Focus & Energy Tracking
- Support focused work sessions.
- Record personal energy levels.
- Reflect on productivity patterns.
- Develop better awareness of daily performance.

## 💰 Personal Finance Management

Lumina also includes a dedicated money management system that helps users understand and organise their finances.

### 1. Budget Management
- Create monthly budgets.

### 2. Income Tracking
- Record income transactions.
- Categorise financial entries.
- Maintain a record of incoming money.

### 3. Expense Tracking
- Record daily expenses.
- Classify expenses by category.
- Monitor spending activity.
- Review where money is going.

### 4. Financial Overview
- View financial activity.
- Monitor income and expenditure.
- Understand spending patterns.
- Make more informed budgeting decisions.

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

Lumina is built around the idea that personal growth and financial well-being are connected.

Building better habits, managing time effectively, achieving goals, and understanding spending patterns all contribute to a more intentional life.

Rather than separating these activities across multiple applications, Lumina brings them together in one experience.

## 🔐 Privacy & Data Storage

Lumina is designed around local device storage.

- Personal activity data is stored locally on the device.
- Financial records are managed locally.
- Habits, goals, tasks, and transactions are maintained within the application's local data environment.

Users should keep their devices secure and maintain appropriate backups of important information.
